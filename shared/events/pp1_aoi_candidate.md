# PP1 Candidate AOI - Rationale

**AOI ID:** `PP1_AOI_KELANI_01`
**Status:** Candidate / Provisional
**Purpose:** Progress Presentation 1 (PP1) common study window

## 1. Purpose of the candidate AOI

This rectangle is a **candidate PP1 study window**, not a flood boundary and not a claim that the entire area was flooded during either candidate event.

It was defined as a common, manageable spatial frame for investigating the two candidate Kelani-related flood events selected for PP1. The intention is to provide a consistent spatial context for data preparation, flood-label generation, modelling, evaluation, and comparison across the four project components.

The candidate window follows the proposal's event-window concept, in which historical flood events are analysed using spatial windows of approximately **60 × 60 km where broader context is required**, subject to the actual flood extent and availability of suitable multimodal data.

## 2. Why this box was selected

The candidate rectangle provides a practical initial spatial frame around the Kelani-related study area while retaining sufficient surrounding context for the PP1 proof of concept.

The box is intended to support:

* a common spatial reference for the four components
* consistent grid and label preparation
* inclusion of relevant river and floodplain context rather than only individual flooded locations
* manageable data volumes for the PP1 implementation period
* comparison of flood and non-flood areas within the same spatial framework.

Therefore, the box is a **working hypothesis for the study area**, rather than a final determination of the geographic extent of either flood event.

## 3. Conditions for final AOI confirmation

The candidate AOI must be validated before it is treated as the canonical PP1 study window. Final confirmation is conditional on evidence from:

1. **DMC-reported affected locations** - whether documented affected districts, Divisional Secretariat areas, settlements, and other reported flood impacts fall within or meaningfully relate to the proposed window.
2. **Relevant river-gauge evidence** - whether available river water-level observations and warnings provide hydrological evidence relevant to the selected study area.
3. **Sentinel-1 coverage** - whether suitable pre-event and flood/post-event acquisitions are available with adequate spatial coverage and compatible acquisition characteristics.
4. **Flood/non-flood spatial variation** - whether the window contains sufficient variation to distinguish flooded and non-flooded areas for meaningful model training and evaluation.
5. **Suitability for all four components** - whether the same spatial frame can support the satellite, weather, environmental, and multimodal-fusion workflows without creating an artificial advantage or limitation for any individual component.

If these checks show that the candidate rectangle is unsuitable or unnecessarily restrictive, the AOI may be adjusted and the change documented.

## 4. PP1 decision principle

The final AOI will therefore be selected based on **event evidence and multimodal data suitability**, rather than convenience alone.

The decision can be defended as:

> **The rectangle was selected as a provisional common study window to provide manageable spatial coverage and sufficient contextual area for the PP1 experiments. It will only be adopted as the final AOI after checking DMC-reported impacts, relevant river-gauge evidence, Sentinel-1 availability, flood/non-flood spatial variation, and compatibility with all four components.**

**Important:** The candidate AOI must not be described as the actual flood extent unless independent flood-reference evidence supports that claim.
