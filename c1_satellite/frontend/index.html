Authentic Satellite Ingestion & Quality Control
Real Copernicus Sentinel-1 SAR and ASCAT Soil Water Index products

Sentinel-1 C-Band SAR Imagery

  • Source: Google Earth Engine (COPERNICUS/S1_GRD)
  • Modes: Interferometric Wide (IW), Ground Range Detected (GRD)
  • Polarizations: Dual-polarization VV and VH backscatter in decibels (dB)
  • Coverage: 204 real gauge-centred 12.8 × 12.8 km patches (256×256 px at 50 m)
  • Quality Gate: Strict 98% patch validity mask — no missing pixels invented
  • Cutoff Rule: Minimum 48-hour availability lag before forecast issue time (09:30 SLST)
  • Multi-Temporal Orbit Matching: Requires identical relative orbit track for temporal differencing

Copernicus ASCAT Soil Water Index (SWI V4)

  • Source: Copernicus Global Land Monitoring Service (CGLS) Daily SWI Version 4
  • Downloaded Range: 08 November – 26 December 2025 (49 daily raster files)
  • Bands Used: SWI005 (characteristic time T=5 days) and SWI010 (T=10 days)
  • Grid Resolution: 0.1° (~12.5 km × 12.5 km) global grid over Sri Lanka
  • Quality Threshold: Filtered strictly to Quality Flag QFLAG ≥ 50%
  • Calibration: Decoded with ×0.5 factor from raw uint8 (0–200) to 0–100% saturation
  • Availability Rule: Fixed 2-day operational lag (D-2) to ensure authentic product publication

Research Methodology, Integrity & PP1 Boundaries
FloodRisk Sri Lanka (J26-DS-314) Component 1 Specification

Research Objective

Component 1 investigates whether augmenting traditional gauging station river-stage observations with multi-temporal satellite radar backscatter (Sentinel-1 SAR) and satellite-derived soil moisture indices (ASCAT SWI) provides measurable improvements in 24-hour river stage forecasts.

Scientific Data Integrity Guarantees

  • Zero Future Target Leakage: Temporal stage history uses only D-1 and D-2 observations; target stage is never seen during inference.
  • Chronological Data Splits: Fixed partition of Train (17 Nov - 09 Dec), Validation (10 - 16 Dec), and Test (17 - 25 Dec) avoids temporal contamination.
  • Authentic Raw Records: No synthetic labels or fabricated satellite imagery.
  • Strict Satellite Latency Rules: Assumes a 48-hour SAR publication delay and 2-day SWI latency before 09:30 SLST forecast issuance.

Key Scientific Findings (PP1)

During the nationwide recession period of late 2025, water stages receded smoothly. The simple zero-change persistence baseline is exceptionally strong (0.2182 m MAE). Model A matches this benchmark while demonstrating superior adaptability across 84.4% of river basins compared to pure gauge-history models.

Honest Limitations & Disclaimers

  • Research Prototype: Designed as an empirical investigation tool for academic Viva evaluation, not an operational civilian warning system.
  • Test Set Caution: The 17–25 Dec test partition was previously inspected in exploratory phases and is treated as exploratory only.
  • Recession Regime Bias: True multi-temporal satellite value is expected to show highest divergence during major rapid-rising monsoon storm events.
